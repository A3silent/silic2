Silic2 Phase 0 baseline
========================

Command:
  ./silic2 res/maps/test_room.json --seed 42

Environment:
  macOS, Debug build, test_room.json

Expected deterministic fields:
  seed=42
  initial enemies=1
  enemy type=chaser
  enemy hp=3
  restart keeps seed=42
  restart increments run_index

Expected lifecycle:
  Room clear records reason=completed and rooms_cleared=1.
  Contact death records reason=player_died and last_damage_source=enemy_contact.
  A successful shot increments shots_fired once.
  A bullet increments shots_hit at most once.
  Restart clears shots, hits, kills, damage, bullets, impact lights, and particles.
  Run and room time do not advance while paused.

Timing, FPS, player input, accuracy, and distance are intentionally not fixed
baseline values.
