Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one square, opaque, tileable pixel-art albedo texture for a 320x200 first-person dark alien orbital-combat game. This is a flat material tile, not an illustration of an object or room.
Style: deliberately authored 64-by-64 logical pixel design enlarged cleanly to the output image. Large quiet color clusters, roughly 6 muted value steps, low local contrast; details must survive final reduction to 64x64. Use crisp pixel clusters rather than photographic noise.
Projection: orthographic flat top-down material swatch filling every edge. Even unlit base color, no baked shadows, highlights, perspective, bevel lighting, lens effects or background.
Tiling: make left/right and top/bottom transitions consistent and unobtrusive; avoid a visible frame and avoid seams running exactly around the tile perimeter. Do not create a repeating brick wall grid. All edges should continue the same material naturally.
Constraints: original design; no lettering, symbols, numerals, logos, border, watermark, props, organisms or character parts. No bright white/black contrast, glints, neon, isolated one-pixel noise, dense scratches, checkerboard or dithering.
Material: pale mineralized ceramic alloy used on enormous leaning supports and low covers. Warm gray/ivory with cool gray mineral bloom, several broad shallow cracks and soft-edged erosion patches. Muted middle values rather than bright white. Broad calm areas. This is an alien architectural material, no bones, skulls, flesh or anatomical ornament. Avoid marble veining and repeated square tiles.
