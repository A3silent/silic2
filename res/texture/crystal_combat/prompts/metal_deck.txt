Use case: stylized-concept

Style: deliberately authored 64-by-64 logical pixel design enlarged cleanly to the output image. Large quiet color clusters, roughly 6 muted value steps, low local contrast; details must survive final reduction to 64x64. Use crisp pixel clusters rather than photographic noise.

Constraints: original design; no lettering, symbols, numerals, logos, border, watermark, props, organisms or character parts. No bright white/black contrast, glints, neon, isolated one-pixel noise, dense scratches, checkerboard or dithering.
Material: worn dark slate-gray orbital alloy deck plates. A few broad interlocking plate masses with restrained shallow, wide joints; one or two broad rubbed patches and quiet scuff groups. Mostly mid-dark neutral blue-gray, no orange hazard paint, bolts or gratings. The tile should read as worn metal across wide floors and slopes without dominating enemies. Large uninterrupted plate areas occupy most of the image.
