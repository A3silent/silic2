Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one square, opaque, tileable pixel-art albedo texture for a 320x200 first-person dark alien orbital-combat game. This is a flat material tile, not an illustration of an object or room.
Style: deliberately authored 64-by-64 logical pixel design enlarged cleanly to the output image. Large quiet color clusters, roughly 6 muted value steps, low local contrast; details must survive final reduction to 64x64. Use crisp pixel clusters rather than photographic noise.

Constraints: original design; no lettering, symbols, numerals, logos, border, watermark, props, organisms or character parts. No bright white/black contrast, glints, neon, isolated one-pixel noise, dense scratches, checkerboard or dithering.
Material: cold blue-gray opaque crystal interior cleavage. A small number of broad angular planes separated by subdued dark split lines, slight cloudy mineral inclusions inside calm planes. Low saturation, middle-dark blue-teal/slate, limited values. No glowing veins, gems, bright diamonds, transparency, reflections, sparkles or neon. Existing game shader will provide all lighting and temporal emission; this image must remain unlit and subdued.
